### Printf Function
```C
printf("format String",Auguments)
//前面的是输出的具体内容，后面对应于specifier的变量值
"""
some specifiers:
    int-- %d     float-- %f
    single character-- %c    string-- %s
    /n means change to the next line
"""

/// for example
printf("My name is Jeckson. I'm %d years old now and I weigh %f kg.", 12, 56.7)
```
